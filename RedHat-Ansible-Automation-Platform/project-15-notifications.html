Notifications

Making sure a failed nightly health check actually wakes someone up, instead of quietly sitting in Job history.

1. The Production Story

Day 15 at ABC Bank. Three nights into our new Schedule (Project 14), the health check silently failed at 11 PM — a disk filled up — and nobody noticed until Priya happened to check the Jobs screen the next morning. Our Team Lead is unimpressed: "A failure nobody sees is worse than no automation at all — at least a manual process gets watched." Today we fix this permanently with a Notification that posts straight into the team's Slack channel the moment a job fails.

2. Why Notifications Are a Separate Object

A Notification Template is defined once — "post to this Slack channel" or "email this distribution list" — and then attached to any number of Job Templates, Workflows, Projects, or even the whole Organization, each choosing which event (Success, Failure, or Approval-related) should trigger it. Just like Credentials, this is a "configure once, reuse everywhere" pattern.

3. The Notifications Screen

Where: Resources → Notifications. Individual templates/workflows then have their own "Notifications" tab where you attach an existing Notification Template to specific trigger events. Who uses it: Automation Admins.

4. Notification Types We'll Use

Slack

Posts a message to a channel via an incoming webhook — what we'll use today for immediate team visibility.

Email

Sends to a distribution list — useful for formal, retained records auditors sometimes ask for.

Webhook

A generic HTTP POST to any URL — used later to integrate with ServiceNow (Project 26) using the custom Credential Type we built in Project 07.

6. Hands-On: Creating and Attaching a Slack Notification

  • Click Resources → Notifications → Add.
  • Name: Cards-Team-Slack-Alerts; Notification Type: Slack.
  • Token: paste the incoming webhook token from Slack's app settings (created once by an Automation Admin).
  • Channels: #cards-payments-automation. Click Save.
  • Open Resources → Templates → Cards - Linux Health Check → Notifications tab.
  • Under "Failure," click to enable Cards-Team-Slack-Alerts. Leave "Success" off for a routine health check — we don't want a Slack message every single night it passes, only when something's actually wrong.
  • Save. The next time this template fails — scheduled or manual — the channel gets an immediate post with a link straight to the Job Output.
Production tipBe deliberate about which events trigger a notification. Attaching "Success" on every routine job trains the team to ignore the channel entirely — reserve proactive alerts for Failure and, where relevant, Approval requests.

10. Interview Prep — Say This Out Loud

"How does a Notification Template relate to a Job Template in AAP?"

A Notification Template — like a Slack or email config — is defined once and then attached to any number of Job Templates, Workflows, or Projects, with each one choosing which specific event (Success, Failure, Approval) should trigger it. It's the same reusable, configure-once pattern as Credentials.

"Why wouldn't you enable a Success notification on every routine job?"

It trains the team to ignore the channel through alert fatigue. Reserving notifications for genuine failures or approval requests keeps the channel meaningful and actionable.

11. Wrap-Up

Key Learnings

  • Notification Templates are defined once and reused across many Templates/Workflows.
  • Be deliberate about Success vs Failure triggers to avoid alert fatigue.
  • Webhook-type notifications are how we'll later integrate ServiceNow.
